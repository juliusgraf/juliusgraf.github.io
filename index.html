Julius Graf

Ph.D. Student at UC Berkeley IEOR

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Industrial Engineering and Operations Research (IEOR) at UC Berkeley, advised by Thibaut Mastrolia. Before beginning the Ph.D., I earned an M.Eng. in IEOR from UC Berkeley and a Diplôme d'Ingénieur from CentraleSupélec.

My research interests include financial mathematics, stochastic optimal control, and reinforcement learning. My current work focuses on:

  • Stochastic control methods for financial systems under cyber risk and partial information
  • Computational and learning-based approaches to Hawkes-process models of market order flow
